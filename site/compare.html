Breakra and oasdiff, honestly.

oasdiff is the mature open-source OpenAPI diff tool, and we use it to cross-check Breakra in CI. Here's where each one fits.

Which should you use?

Choose oasdiff

When you want the broadest rule coverage, a ready-made GitHub Action, changelogs, or a free hosted MCP server.

Choose Breakra

When an agent needs one decision it can act on (safe/review/block), with evidence and a fix for every finding, honest unknowns, and pay-per-call without an account.

Where they disagree, on purpose

Our CI runs oasdiff v1.33.0 on the same cases and fails on any undocumented disagreement. Breakra is deliberately stricter in two places:

  • Authentication added to an operation: oasdiff rates it INFO; Breakra rates it potentially breaking, because unauthenticated callers will be rejected.
  • Optional response field removed: oasdiff rates it INFO; Breakra rates it potentially breaking, because consumers that read it may break.

Every difference is listed with its reason in tests/oracle-differences.json.

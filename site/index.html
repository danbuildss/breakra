FAQ

Is it really deterministic?

Yes. Findings come from versioned rules and the evidence in your contracts; there's no AI in the check. The same inputs always give the same result and analysis_id.

Where do my contracts go?

Locally (CLI or MCP): nowhere. Hosted: to Bankr x402 Cloud (and Vercel for the breakra.dev forward). Contracts are never stored or logged. Don't send contracts you aren't allowed to share.

What does "unknown" mean?

A change the rules don't evaluate. It's never treated as safe: the verdict becomes review.

How is it different from oasdiff?

oasdiff is the mature open-source diff tool, with more rules and a free hosted MCP. Breakra focuses on a single agent-ready decision (safe/review/block), evidence and a fix per finding, honest unknowns, usage filtering, and pay-per-call without an account. We cross-check against oasdiff in CI. Full comparison →

What can't it do (yet)?

It doesn't read your code: --use filters contract findings to the endpoints you list. Very large specs (like Stripe's) exceed its limits. It doesn't fetch URLs or external $refs.
